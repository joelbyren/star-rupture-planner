  3. Brass & BoilerworksSteampunk · sepia Victorian engineering office — riveted brass plates, copper pipework edges, ledger side panel.
  4. Orbital Ops HolotableSci-fi · hologram Starship planning table — cyan wireframe holo-panels, flowing conduit edges, HUD chrome.
  5. Graphite AtelierMinimal · ember accent Editorial grayscale minimalism — warm near-black, hairline rules, serif headlines, floating chrome, a single ember accent on the target.
  6. Blackline ConsoleBrutalist · acid accent Utilitarian mono console — true black, dot grid, stamped index-card nodes, orthogonal traces, raw-feed ticker, one acid-green accent.
  7. Freight ManifestPrint · light theme Dispatch-office packing sheet — paper and ink, safety-yellow accents, hazard stripes, stamped shipping-label nodes.
